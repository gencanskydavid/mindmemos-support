Mind Memos Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Developer: David Gencansky

In short: Mind Memos does not collect your data. Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and tasks stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What Mind Memos stores on your iPhone

When you use the app it keeps the following on your device only: meeting recordings (audio files), transcripts, summaries, action items and tasks, the search index and knowledge graph built from them, and your settings. Recordings are saved in the app's Recordings folder, which you can also see in the Files app under On My iPhone › Mind Memos. None of this is uploaded to the developer or to any server.

How your recordings are processed

Everything that turns audio into notes runs on your iPhone: speech recognition (Apple's on-device speech recognition and an on-device Parakeet model for Czech and Slovak), speaker detection, language detection, summaries and action items (a Gemma model that runs locally, or Apple Intelligence where your iPhone has it), and search (on-device embedding and re-ranking models). Your audio and text are not sent to any AI service.

Network use

Mind Memos connects to the internet for one purpose: downloading the on-device models listed above. The downloads come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). You start the larger ones yourself from Settings; a small language-detection model (about 75 MB) is downloaded in the background the first time you record. Like any download, these requests reveal your IP address and standard technical information to the host. No recordings, transcripts, summaries or personal data are included. Mind Memos does not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services and contains no third-party tracking code.

iOS itself may download language assets for speech recognition or translation from Apple. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone to record your meetings. Recording continues while the screen is locked or the app is in the background, and a Live Activity shows that a recording is running.
  • Speech Recognition to transcribe the recording on your device.
  • Notifications to remind you of task deadlines. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

You can change these at any time in iOS Settings.

Sharing that you control

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it. Export Data opens the iOS share sheet with your notes as text. Auto-Email Transcript opens the system Mail composer so you can review the message and send it yourself. Files in the Recordings folder are available to you through the Files app and Finder.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Delete a meeting in the Meetings tab to remove its recording, transcript, summary and related items. Deleting the app removes everything Mind Memos stored on the device. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing on a server to delete or export on request.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations differ by country and situation. You are responsible for telling people they are being recorded and for getting any consent that applies.

Children

Mind Memos is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. A change that sends any of your data off the device would be described here and in the app before it takes effect.
