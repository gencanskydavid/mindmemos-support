Questions

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later. Gemma 4 summaries need a lot of memory; Mind Memos was tested on an iPhone 16. Where your iPhone has Apple Intelligence, Mind Memos can use it for summaries instead.

Why is there nothing to download or sign in to?

Mind Memos has no account. The AI models it uses are downloaded once, from Settings, and then everything runs offline. Use Wi-Fi: the Gemma 4 model is about 4 GB.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, French, Spanish, Czech, Slovak, Japanese and Korean. Choose one in Settings, or leave Automatic to let Mind Memos detect it.

Where are my recordings?

In the Files app under On My iPhone › Mind Memos › Recordings.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a meeting to delete it in the Meetings tab, or delete the app to remove everything. See the privacy policy.

A recording or summary did not finish.

Summaries with Gemma 4 need Mind Memos open on screen; if you leave the app, the meeting waits and finishes when you come back.

Models and open-source software

Mind Memos is built with open-source software and openly published models, each under its own licence: Apple's Speech and Foundation Models frameworks, MLX and MLX Swift, FluidAudio, WhisperKit, Google Gemma, NVIDIA Parakeet, BAAI BGE-M3 and BGE-Reranker. Thanks to their authors.
